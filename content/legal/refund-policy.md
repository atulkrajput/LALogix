## 2. Cancelling a subscription

- You can cancel at any time by writing to [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}) or messaging us on WhatsApp at {{phone}}.
- Your subscription stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for. It will not renew after that.
- Cancelling does not by itself create a refund for the current period, except in the cases below.

## 3. When we refund

We refund the amount paid in these cases:

- **Duplicate payment:** you were charged twice for the same invoice.
- **Charged after cancelling:** you were charged for a renewal after you had cancelled in time.
- **Service not delivered:** we could not activate the product you paid for.
- **First payment, early request:** you ask for a refund within 7 days of your first payment for a product, and the account has not been used for regular business work.

Set-up or data-loading charges for work already done, and amounts for periods already used, are not refundable.

## 4. How to ask for a refund

Write to [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}) with your name, registered mobile number, product name, payment date and the payment or invoice number. We reply within 3 working days.

## 5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method (card, UPI, net banking or bank account) within 5–7 working days of approval. Your bank may take a few more days to show the amount. Where a GST invoice was issued, we issue a matching credit note.
